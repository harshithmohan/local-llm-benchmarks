that variant (gates /5 + rubric /95 = total /100); Overall = mean of the variant

2-repeat means. Per-repeat rubric breakdown in `scorecards/<model>.md`; run artifacts
under `runs/<model>/<variant>/` are deleted once recorded — the scorecards are the
persistent record.
