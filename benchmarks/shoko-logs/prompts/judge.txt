## Reviewer notes

<prose>

**Bottom line**: <prose>

**Score A - Ticket conformance** | **<n> / 100**
**Score B - Robustness & fidelity** | **<n> / 100**

Those two lines are the green-build totals. They apply when the build row shows
a tick: each <n> is the points earned over that section's lines, and the
denominator is exactly 100.

When the build row shows a cross the build is red, and the two totals change
shape. Write the shape that matches the build you were given.

On a red build the Score A total is exactly N/G, with no number and no
denominator, so the Score A line is exactly:
**Score A - Ticket conformance** | **N/G**

On a red build the Score B total is the points still earned over the lines a red
build could still grade, with that reduced maximum as the denominator: both
numbers present, the denominator below 100, and the numerator at or below it.
The Score B line is then exactly:
**Score B - Robustness & fidelity** | **<n> / <m>**

for example 74 / 82, because B2 and B11 are the only Score B lines a red build
cannot grade. A red-build Score B total of N/G, or a Score B denominator of 100,
is an error, and a red-build Score A total of anything but N/G is an error.

Score tokens are exactly one of 0, 0.5, 1, N, G.
0.5 is only legal on A1 A2 A4 A5 A6 A7 A8 A10 A12 A13 A16 A17 A18 A19 and on
B1 B2 B4 B5 B6 B9 B11. N is only legal when the build is red; G is only legal on
a red build.
