# Rig 2: RTX 3090 + Ryzen 7 5700X

- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 24GB (compute 8.6)
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 5700X (8c/16t, tests used `--threads 8`)
- RAM: 32 GB DDR4-3200
- VRAM budget: 22 GB +- 250 MB for llama.cpp (desktop system, not headless)
- Stock binary: upstream llama-server (upstream 0.4.0-dev, build 10809, commit 5266f24da7)
- Codacus fork ([github.com/thecodacus/llama.cpp](https://github.com/thecodacus/llama.cpp)): build b10818-27c54b4bb.
- Models are referenced as `<models>/` in the configs below; routing profiles at `<models>/moe-cache-profiles/`
- Benchmark results: [models/rig2-3090/qwen36-35b-a3b.md](models/rig2-3090/qwen36-35b-a3b.md), [models/rig2-3090/qwen38-27b.md](models/rig2-3090/qwen38-27b.md)
